I read the scheduler, the gate, the selftest coupling, and the state/ignore layout to ground this. Fourteen decisions I'd take back before writing a plan.

---

1. **What the retrospective audit is required to *output*.** The criterion contains exactly one decision rule — the M1ᵗ→arm table — and the operator amendment forbids using it ("this slice selects no arm"). "The corpus *narrows* the arms" has no operational definition anywhere: no rule states what evidence eliminates an arm or what a narrowing looks like on the page, so the slice's central deliverable currently has no pass/fail shape.

2. **What arm B ("reshape") concretely denotes.** #590 already deleted the only non-model spawn, and the two surviving sites are BUILD (`orchestrate-lean.sh:800`) and REVIEW (`:929`) — both invoke payload skills that unambiguously need a model session. "Keeps only the spawns which genuinely need a model session and replaces the rest with direct `lean-gate.sh` calls" has an empty target set as written, so the 0.50–0.80 band currently selects an arm with no content.

3. **Whether the corpus and its derived numbers can be committed at all, and what a reviewer's oracle is if not.** Spawn logs live at `$MAIN_ROOT/$STATE_DIR` (`orchestrate-lean.sh:377-378`) under `.claude/pipeline-state/`, which `.gitignore` excludes; `cost-log.jsonl` sits in the same ignored dir and lean takes a documented *skip* rather than writing a row (`lean-gate.sh:4870`). AC-2's "committed evidence file" and AC-8 therefore rest on artifacts absent from every clean checkout and unre-derivable by anyone but the author — on a public repo where personal-machine paths are denylisted.

4. **Whether one corpus row is a launch or a spawn.** A single launch contains up to `--max-continuations` BUILD spawns, a REVIEW spawn, and a bounded REVIEW retry; M1ᵗ is defined per *launch* while its class-T evidence ("spawn log ends mid-milestone") is per *spawn*, and B2's own finding is that `SPAWN_N` resets per process and `: > "$log"` (`:621`) destroys the prior launch's spawn-1 transcript. A launch with three spawns, two clean and one class-T, has no stated score.

5. **Who assigns the T/P/S/X/U class, and whether anyone re-classifies blind.** Class X is declared "not a lane failure and never counted against the scheduler," so every judgment call between "the transport lost the signal" and "the work was wrong" moves M1ᵗ directly. The criterion fixes the rubric but never names an adjudicator, while conceding the author personally lived the corpus.

6. **Where a killed session lands in the rubric.** The five classes are turn-ended-unmet, tool denial, machine slept, content, and unattributable — and #617's cited incident (two sessions killed mid-milestone-3, invisible at the terminal) matches none of them cleanly. This lane's `kill -9`/infra-death shape is common enough that `orchestrate-lean.sh` carries a dedicated `infra_token()` read for it (`:752`), so it is not an edge case.

7. **Whether runs whose subject *was* the lane stay in the corpus, and unweighted.** The window opens at #548 — the ticket that added spawn logging to `orchestrate-lean.sh` itself — and the 08-16→08-22 stretch is heavy with transport-repair tickets, which is the population most likely to exhibit class-T interventions. The no-drop rule forbids excluding them and nothing weights them.

8. **How operator-attention minutes — the criterion's declared *primary* metric — is obtained for a retrospective corpus.** B3 fixes a protocol only for the manual arm, and arm-A spawn logs carry no attention instrumentation at all. Either the audit is decided on the metric the criterion calls secondary, or attention is wholly #650's and this slice's instrument is missing its primary channel.

9. **The exact text and bar of AC-7 and AC-8.** Both are named in the amendment and stated nowhere, and milestone 1 parses numbered `AC-n` out of the committed spec (`lean-gate.sh:3326-3328`), so their wording is gate-visible rather than cosmetic. AC-8 as glossed — "every corpus spawn carries its evidence" — reads as directly contradicted by B2's finding that earlier launches' transcripts are destroyed.

10. **Whether the criterion is a separate file under `docs/plans/` or a section of the gate-parsed spec.** AC-1 demands it in the slice's *first* commit; milestone 1 requires `docs/plans/second-shift-643-lean.md` to carry numbered ACs plus a ledger-lint-clean Decision Ledger, and the amendment's `D-1`/`D-2`/`D-3` already live in that file. One file or two changes what milestone 1 sees and whether a ~200-line criterion body has to survive ledger-lint.

11. **Whether arm A's stated scope is true — because this slice commits the document that asserts it.** "Arm A remains scoped to the scheduler layer only — `build-lean`, `review-lean` and `lean-gate.sh` are out of scope under every arm" is false as written: `scenario-liveness-selftest.sh:1378-1383` executes the real `orchestrate-lean.sh` and hard-fails when it is absent, `lean-gate.sh` references it, and rows keyed to it sit in `tools/mutation-catalog.tsv` (4), `mutation-baseline.tsv` (1), `mutation-pair-map.tsv` (1), `selftest-suite-timings.tsv` (1) and `docs/prose-blocker-triage.tsv` (4).

12. **Whether this slice makes tracker writes, and under whose identity.** AC-7 makes "the follow-up is filed" a criterion of this slice, while the amendment already cites #650 by number and states #617/#638/#639 were "re-pointed by comment on each" as done. `run-lean`'s own contract is that the scheduler authors nothing and every write belongs to a payload block under the bot identity.

13. **Whether AC-1's "landed before any timing is collected" can bind a corpus that predates the ticket.** All 57 logs fall in 2026-08-16→08-22; the criterion itself demotes any claim over that window to retrodiction carrying "no evidentiary weight." If the audit's own numbers inherit that demotion, the pre-registration is satisfied in form while the artifact it registers has already been read.

14. **Whether the instrument re-derives the ticket's cost figures or inherits them.** The ~9-minute cold-start tax is a single datapoint (run #546, spawn → first gate call) that also contains the build session's own preflight, and "each phase boundary costs a full gate execution" is measured against read-only `LEAN_GATE_OBSERVE=1` subcommands (`verdict_rc`, `staleness_rc`, `inflight_rc`, `progress`) rather than `bash G all`. Both premises are load-bearing for the arm framing and neither is re-measured by anything in scope.
